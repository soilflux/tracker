The tracker is designed to be used with the browser in fullscreen mode (f11).

Inputs:
All inputs are done using an input box listed on the left of each check name. See 'Codes' for more info.

Check Color Codes:
Bright green = can access logically with items you currently have
Faded green = can access logically with items you currently have access to
Faded yellow = can check out of logic (based on items you currently have access to)
White = known but currently unobtainable

Child and adult emoji boxes are used to input spawns relevant to check accessibility for child and adult, respectively (or even irrelevant spawns to help you remember).

Timer can be started and paused using the pause key

Check Summary:
Right clicking locations will toggle the item from it as obtained/unobtained.

Simulator:
To start the simulator click the "Play the Sim" button and select a spoiler log (only S6 and S5 tested).
If you enter in a value from 1-200 in the input box next to the "Play the Sim" button, a seed included with the tracker will be used instead of requiring a selection(this feature does not work offline).
The log will appear which keeps track of what you find. It will initially tell you your starting dungeon reward, song, age, and spawn.
Click the "Check Altar" button to mark medallions and stones.
Click the "Check Adult Spawn" or "Check Child Spawn" button to reveal the spawn for the age you didn't start as.
Left click a check to get it. Right click a check to peek it. You can then left or right click the peeked check again to obtain it.
If you peek a check which is not freestanding, it will tell you that you found an unknown big chest, labeled by "???". You can left click the check again to obtain the item there.
Click a Gossip Stone check (colored pink) to get a hint. WotH, Path, Barren, Always, and Sometimes hints can then be manually entered in the tracker.

Animal Companionship:
Gain 1 companion XP with current animal after inputting 20 items into check input boxes or through the hint box.
companion level is equal to 2.5*sqrt of companion XP-1, rounded down.
the tracker will roll a d2 once for every companion level, then reroll animals other than your highest level animal for every result of 1. if it fails to roll that animal, it will repeat the process for your next highest level animal.
in other words, increase rolling odds for a particular animal after 1,2,3,4,6,8,11,13,16,20... seeds played with that animal.
wish for: the wished for animal will be given highest rolling priority and a 2 lvl buff towards its chance of being rolled. this eventually guarantees its dominance even if you don't consistently refresh for it.

Useful info has been placed on the edges of the tracker to optimize around playing with an emulator over the center of the tracker.

Songs are now in the main checklist, mainly to make the next check highlight more intuitive.

New input methods, see halp for more info. The short of it is that everything can be done through the boxes next to checks. The boxes for always/sometimes hints, barrens, paths, entrances, and medallions are now optional, slower alternatives.

Shortened item inputs, and added faster alternatives. All of the old ones will still work, although many of them will trigger with only 2 letters, which may throw you off for a bit.

Items next to dungeon names now show items you don't have, in order from most checks unlocked in that dungeon to least.

A new layout for information on songs has been added.
-A green background means the vanilla check for that song is accessible and unknown.
-A red background means the vanilla check for that song is inaccessible and unknown.
-The pictures show whether you have the item or not, as usual.
